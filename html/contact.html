FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about WorkBandhu.

Is WorkBandhu free to use?

Yes! Creating an account and browsing workers is completely free for customers. Workers can also register for free.

How are workers verified?

All workers must submit a valid ID during registration. Verification is reviewed before a worker's profile becomes visible.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. You can cancel from your dashboard. We ask that you cancel at least 4 hours before the scheduled job start time.

How are the daily rates set?

Workers set their own daily rates. All rates are displayed publicly on their profile before you book.

What if I have a problem with a worker?

Use the Contact form above to report any issue. We take all complaints seriously and will respond within 24 hours.

Is WorkBandhu a college project?

Yes! WorkBandhu is a 4-member college project. The platform is fully functional as a frontend demonstration.

A simple, fair way to connect daily wage workers with people who need them.
