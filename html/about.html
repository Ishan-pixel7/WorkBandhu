Our Story

Built for Fair, Everyday Work.

WorkBandhu exists to remove the uncertainty daily wage workers face every morning, and the guesswork employers face when they need reliable help fast.

Our Mission

Why We Started WorkBandhu

In most cities, daily wage workers gather at informal labour points hoping for work, with no guarantee of pay rate, safety, or even being picked that day. WorkBandhu bridges that gap with a transparent platform where both sides know exactly what to expect.

🎯

Transparent Platform

All rates, ratings, and availability shown upfront, no hidden costs or surprises.

🤝

Fair for Workers Too

Community First
