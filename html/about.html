Our Story

Built For Fair, Everyday Work.

WorkBandhu exists to remove the uncertainty daily wage workers face every morning, and the guesswork employers face when they need reliable help fast.

The Problem

Why We Started WorkBandhu

In most cities, daily wage workers gather at informal labour points hoping for work, with no guarantee of pay rate, safety, or even being picked that day. Employers, meanwhile, struggle to find verified, skilled workers on short notice. WorkBandhu bridges that gap with a simple online platform where both sides know exactly what to expect.

What We Value

Our Principles

Fair Pay

Transparent daily rates agreed upfront no last minute renegotiation.

Verified Workers

Every profile includes ratings and history from real past bookings.

Simple for Everyone

No jargon, no complicated steps built to work for first-time internet users too.

The Team

A College Project, Built With Care

WorkBandhu is a 4-member college project exploring how technology can make daily wage hiring more dignified and efficient. The team is passionate about creating a platform that benefits both workers and employers, and we hope to continue improving it with your feedback.

A simple, fair way to connect daily wage workers with people who need them.
